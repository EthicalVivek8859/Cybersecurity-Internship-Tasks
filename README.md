## 📝 Documented Tasks

### Task 2: Pre-Engagement Report & Threat Modeling
This milestone focuses on the foundational phase of a penetration testing lifecycle. Proper scoping and planning are critical before launching any active security assessment. The uploaded PDF report includes:
* **Authorization & Scope:** Defining the legal boundaries and explicit permissions required to conduct security testing on the target infrastructure.
* **Asset Inventory:** A structured mapping of target assets, specifically identifying the local training web app (LAB-01) and deliberately vulnerable VM (LAB-02), alongside their specific IP addresses, testing conditions, and environments.
* **Data-Flow Diagram (DFD):** A visual representation of how data moves across the target network, helping to identify potential trust boundaries, processes, and attack surfaces.
* **STRIDE Threat Model:** A comprehensive register analyzing potential threats based on Spoofing, Tampering, Repudiation, Information Disclosure, Denial of Service, and Elevation of Privilege. It also outlines robust mitigation strategies for each identified vulnerability.
* **Rules of Engagement (RoE):** Established strict testing windows, evidence handling procedures, escalation paths, and stop conditions (such as lab crashes) to ensure safe execution.
